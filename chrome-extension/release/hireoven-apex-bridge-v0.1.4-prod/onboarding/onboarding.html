Hireoven Apex Bridge

Before you start

Apex Bridge autofills job applications and detects listings on any ATS or careers page you visit — which means it can read the page you're on and, when you use autofill, your Hireoven profile data. Here's exactly what that means.

What it accesses
The page you're on — to recognize a job listing or an application form. Pages that aren't a job listing or application are ignored; nothing is read or sent.
Your Hireoven profile — name, contact info, work authorization, education, résumé, and any voluntary EEOC/demographic answers you've saved — used only to fill matching fields on an application you open.
What you type into a form — so a manual correction is remembered and reused next time, instead of asking again.
What never happens
  • Data goes only to Hireoven's own servers (hireoven.com) — never to a third party, never sold, never used for ads.
  • Nothing is submitted automatically. You review every autofilled field before you hit submit.
  • Uninstalling removes everything stored locally; deleting your Hireoven account removes everything stored on our servers.
